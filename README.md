# 🎮 FrameHeist

### A smoother picture for your console games

**🌐 English · [🇩🇪 Hier geht’s zur deutschen Anleitung](README.de.md)**

Want smoother-looking gameplay on your PS5 or Xbox? FrameHeist shows you how to bring your console's picture to a PC and use **Lossless Scaling** to add extra frames — with the aim of turning 30 FPS into a smoother 60 FPS or more.

GTA VI is the focus of this project, but the same idea can also be used with other console games.

## ✨ The idea in a nutshell

A capture card brings the console's picture to your PC. The free app **vicash** displays it, and **Lossless Scaling** adds intermediate frames to make movement look smoother. This is called *frame generation*.

You play on the monitor connected to your PC. Your controller stays connected to the console.

The extra frames improve how smooth the picture looks. They do not make the console itself run faster, and some extra delay or picture errors can occur.

## 🧰 What you need

- **PS5 or Xbox Series X|S**
- **HDMI capture card** and matching HDMI and USB cables
- **Windows PC with a suitable graphics card** — [Lossless Scaling recommends](https://store.steampowered.com/app/993090/Lossless_Scaling/) NVIDIA RTX 30 series, AMD RX 6000 series or Intel Arc
- **Monitor connected to your PC**, with at least 60 Hz
- **[vicash](https://github.com/caaatto/vicash/releases)** — free, displays the console picture on your PC
- **[Lossless Scaling](https://store.steampowered.com/app/993090/Lossless_Scaling/)** — available to buy on Steam
